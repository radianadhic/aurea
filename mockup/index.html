Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know about AUREA. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our team.

How is AUREA different from traditional MDM platforms (Informatica, IBM InfoSphere, etc.)? +

Traditional MDM platforms focus on data consolidation and quality. AUREA adds 4 built-in AI engines (Auto-Insights, Smart Segmentation, Churn Watch, Fraud Detection) that work 24/7 on your master data. This means your MDM investment pays for itself faster — instead of just being a "system of record," AUREA becomes a "system of intelligence." Pricing is also 60-70% lower than legacy MDM suites.

How long does implementation take? +

Typical implementation timeline: 2-4 weeks for Starter plan (cloud, single data domain), 6-8 weeks for Enterprise (multi-domain, on-prem option), and 3-6 months for Custom (tier-1 banks, full regulatory certification). We provide a dedicated implementation team and pre-built connectors for 100+ core banking systems, ERP, and CRM platforms.

Can AUREA be deployed on-premise for regulatory compliance? +

Yes. The Custom plan and on-premise Enterprise deployment run entirely within your data center or private cloud (AWS GovCloud, Azure for Government, Alibaba Cloud Indonesia). No data ever leaves your infrastructure. We also support air-gapped deployments for the most sensitive environments. All deployments are UU PDP Indonesia, GDPR, and PCI-DSS compliant.

What AI/ML models does AUREA use? Are they pre-trained or custom? +

All 4 AI engines come with pre-trained models tuned for Indonesian banking patterns (BI-FAST, QRIS, local fraud patterns, Indonesian customer behavior). For tier-1 banks with unique data, we offer custom model training on your historical data — typically improving accuracy by 15-30% over the baseline. Models are continuously retrained weekly with new data.

How does pricing scale? Are there hidden costs? +

No hidden costs. Pricing is based on: (1) number of records managed, (2) number of AI engines enabled, (3) deployment model (cloud vs on-prem). Starter starts at $2,500/mo, Enterprise at $8,500/mo. Implementation services are quoted separately (typically $15-50K depending on complexity). Volume discounts available for multi-year contracts. See pricing details →

What kind of support and SLAs do you offer? +

Starter: Email support, 24h SLA for P1. Enterprise: Dedicated CSM, 1h SLA for P1, 4h for P2, business-hours chat. Custom: 15-min P0 SLA, 24/7 on-call rotation, named support engineers, quarterly business reviews. All plans include 99.9% uptime SLA (Custom: 99.99%). We also offer on-site training and certification programs for your data team.

Can I try AUREA before buying? +

Yes! Try the live demo right now (no signup required). For a deeper evaluation, we offer a 30-day proof-of-concept with your own data in a sandbox environment — typically delivered in 1 week. Contact our team to schedule a personalized walkthrough with your data and use cases.

How does AUREA handle Indonesian regulations (UU PDP, OJK, BI)? +

AUREA is built for Indonesian regulatory requirements from day one. We support: UU PDP 2022 (data localization, consent management, right to be forgotten), OJK regulations (risk management, consumer protection, financial reporting), Bank Indonesia (open banking, payment systems, LCR/NSFR), and BI-FAST/QRIS native integration. Our team includes ex-OJK and Bank Indonesia consultants.

What happens to my data if I cancel? +

Your data is yours. Upon cancellation, you can export all master data, AI model outputs, and audit logs in standard formats (CSV, JSON, Parquet). We provide a 90-day grace period to migrate, after which data is permanently deleted with a certified destruction certificate. We never hold your data hostage.
